---
name: AI VOD clipper access tier
description: Product entitlement and duration policy for the AI Twitch VOD clipper.
---

The private AI VOD clipper is limited to Admins and Ambassadors, and all eligible private testers receive the Pro-tier allowance. The intended public release is Pro-only. The agreed VOD duration policy is 4 hours for Free and 6 hours for Pro, although Free access is not currently exposed.

**Why:** Ambassadors testing the private feature must not receive a lower “Free” processing limit merely because they do not have a separate paid Pro entitlement.

**How to apply:** Keep private route access limited to Admins/Ambassadors until launch. When public access is enabled, admit Pro users without exposing the feature to Free users, while retaining Admin/Ambassador internal access.
