---
name: Campaign feedback drafts
description: Why autosaved creator feedback must not count as a submitted campaign objective.
---

Campaign feedback being typed or resumed across devices is not staged content. Keep drafts separately from quantity-tracked objective submissions and only mark an objective ready when the creator explicitly saves that response.

**Why:** An autosave that creates a staged submission would silently make the package appear complete while the creator is still writing or has not reviewed their answer.

**How to apply:** When adding other autosaved text objectives, persist their in-progress text independently, scope it to the campaign participant and objective slot, and transition it to staged only on an explicit creator action.
