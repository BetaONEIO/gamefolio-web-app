---
name: Towerdog pixel-art language
description: Visual constraints for the Towerdog Pixel Surge background and foreground effect.
---

Towerdog Pixel Surge should read as restrained retro pixel art: fixed square or rectangular blocks on Gamefolio navy, red activity concentrated left, blue activity right, sparse purple transitions, and stepped activation waves. Avoid gradient-built patterns, diagonal or rotated geometry, stripes, plaid, circles, and dense graph-paper texture.

**Why:** The intended identity depends on crisp, grid-aligned pixels rather than generic neon or argyle effects.

**How to apply:** Keep pixel positions static and animate only opacity, color, timing, and a subtle glow; provide a static sparse square pattern for reduced-motion users and preserve pointer-transparent overlays.

The background landscape uses larger 16–24px tiles on desktop and 12–18px tiles on mobile, with a slower 22-second cycle and roughly 12–18% peak visibility. The smaller foreground wave remains brighter and separate.

**Why:** This keeps the landscape atmospheric rather than competing with the Discord-style profile border and essential profile content.

**How to apply:** Treat large stepped formations as the background terrain, keep the centre sparse, and reserve stronger activation for the smaller foreground overlay.
