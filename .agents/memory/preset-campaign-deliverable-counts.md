name: Preset campaign deliverable counts
description: Why campaign summary totals include optional configured feedback
---

Preset deliverable totals describe all configured objectives, including optional creator feedback. Do not reduce displayed counts to mandatory media only, and do not make optional objectives mandatory just to match the summary.

**Why:** The requested Content Boost setup explicitly presents five deliverables and approximately 25–50 submissions for five–ten creators. Its fifth configured deliverable is feedback; the existing completion rules intentionally make feedback optional.

**How to apply:** Derive display totals and estimates from the canonical preset model throughout selection, finalisation, and review. Preserve the independent mandatory flags that determine package completion.
