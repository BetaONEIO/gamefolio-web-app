---
name: Dark video hero blending
description: Visual treatment for vertical videos with opaque black backgrounds in the Gamefolio-style hero.
---

Use a continuous hero-level gradient that transitions from the page navy into pure black behind the media area. Keep the original video unchanged and avoid making a separate black media container; local radial backdrops tend to expose an oval or column boundary.

**Why:** An opaque black video cannot visually disappear against navy unless the surrounding page reaches the same black value. A hero-wide transition preserves the two-column layout without making the media look carded.

**How to apply:** Put the desktop navy-to-black gradient on the full hero, use a separate vertical gradient on mobile, and reserve any local halo for very low-opacity ambient lighting only.
